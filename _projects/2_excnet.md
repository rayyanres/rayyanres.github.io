Proposed ExCNET, an 8M parameter attention based architecture achieving 97% test accuracy on plant seedling recognition, outperforming heavier models including ResNet-50/152V2, VGG-16/19, DenseNet169, and ViT.

**Tools:** Python, PyTorch, Scikit-Learn
